# Content Guide

Write in a warm, simple and credible voice.

Avoid:

- Guaranteed results
- Medical or mental health claims that are not substantiated
- Fake testimonials
- Attendance numbers or impact statistics that have not been approved

Each program page should eventually include:

- Program introduction
- Audience
- Topics covered
- Format
- Schedule
- Facilitator details, if approved
- Registration or RSVP link
- Frequently asked questions
